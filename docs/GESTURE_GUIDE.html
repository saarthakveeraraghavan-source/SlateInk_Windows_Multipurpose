Fun playground

Disable desktop controls if active, choose Hand touchpad → Touchpad: fun playground, then Enter playground. Portal fire: move the right hand to paint a fiery rope; draw a closed circle to form a portal. Web dome: spread both hands to expand, bring them together to shrink; move the right hand to turn and tilt. Nebula: right hand attracts stars, left hand repels, right thumb/index pinch bursts.

The scene has its own camera hand check. 1/2/3 switches scenes, Space clears, P pauses, F11 switches full screen and Esc / Back returns and stops camera. The Try demo button runs labelled synthetic motion. Desktop clicks, WASD, swipes and Wispr are inactive in Fun. Full playground guide.

Before entry, choose Sound for this scene. Portal fire, Web dome and Nebula have independent saved sound choices, initially off, plus a mute button inside. Fire crackles and seals, the web resonates and chimes, and Nebula has space ambience and a star-burst cascade. Pause, focus loss and exit stop audio.

Floating camera preview — all modes

Check Floating camera preview beside the camera controls and start the camera. The small view stays above other desktop windows in every mode and touchpad sub-mode. It shares the existing mirrored/rotated feed and annotations, without opening a second camera or tracker.

Drag its dark header to place it. Position and on/off choice are saved. It stays on an available screen if monitors change and does not take keyboard focus. Click its × or uncheck the option to hide it. It clears stale/stopped frames. Exclusive-fullscreen games and protected desktops are not validated; windowed/borderless games are the intended desktop-overlay target.

Minimized hand touchpad can keep running with the background option and a visible tray/preview indicator. Other modes can keep their explicitly enabled camera preview while writing, pen/gaze control and pending typing stop. Disabling the only background preview stops its camera session unless the enabled hand touchpad retains its tray indicator.

Main-window – minimizes; a gesture click now releases its owned button before the action. Main-window × / Stop & quit exits and releases the camera. Reopening a second copy brings forward the existing session. Camera acquisition is isolated so a stalled read cannot indefinitely prevent device shutdown. No frames are recorded.

Clicking reliably

Left click remains thumb + index, then release. Other fingers can relax if the contact stays visible beyond the knuckles, as in an OK sign; a resting fist is excluded. It does not require fully opening the middle pinch. A clearly folded index with a straight middle resolves a close joined-finger left pinch; unclear shapes remain blocked.

Right click remains thumb + middle, then release, with the index pinch open. It needs a clear middle target and about 0.1 seconds of steady closure. Left-click needs two contact observations at least 0.025 seconds apart and tolerates small distance jitter. Deliberately moving after holding left still drags/selects. Two left pinches still double-click.

An idle open palm arms the pinch, allowing a click straight from that pose. Clicks are suppressed during a moving swipe and for 0.2 seconds after leaving it. Open the pinch briefly after that interval before clicking.

Swipe to change windows

Use your physical RIGHT hand for both gestures in Touchpad: desktop. Open all four fingers, keep the thumb comfortably away and briefly show the hand before sweeping sideways. Keep your other hand relaxed.

  • Next: follow Next_Swipe-Gesture (2).mp4, sweeping the right hand across your chest toward your left.
  • Previous: follow Previous_Swipe-Gesture (2).mp4, sweeping back toward your right.

With Mirror camera ON, Next moves left in the preview and Previous moves right. Both use your right hand. Mirroring and handedness are corrected automatically; do not reverse the demonstrations yourself.

The same swipe can begin while already moving, including a fast reverse with only one clear starting observation. Two actual end observations remain required. Recognition uses finger-joint depth as well as projection, and keeps new starts during cooldown so a reverse is not discarded.

There is no fist hold or mandatory unlock pose. The persistent cancelled/finished lock has been removed. After interruption, relax/lower the hand and show the open right hand again, or let visible tracking settle. Holding the finish pose does not repeat. The saved cooldown applies between strokes.

Pointer actions pause during an open-hand swipe. Relax the fingers and join index + middle to point again. Tilted two-finger pointing, three-finger scrolling and folded hands cannot start window commands. Active input ownership, physical buttons/modifiers, helper V, dictation and two-palm pause keep priority. Gaming and Drawing never swipe.

Motion blur in the supplied videos loses hand landmarks for several frames. The detector can retain a real start anchor through up to 0.7 seconds of missing landmarks while camera frames remain fresh. It never switches on missing data: two trusted destination observations must confirm whole-palm and wrist travel. Longer loss, stale frames, unsuitable scale changes or ambiguity cancels safely and allows normal recovery.

Uncheck Enable window swipes anytime without stopping the mouse; the choice persists. Disable controls before changing Swipe sensitivity × or Swipe cooldown. Eligible windows cycle in stable order, including minimized windows; SlateInk and unsuitable windows are excluded.

Gaming

Choose Touchpad: gaming. Your physical right hand uses the existing mouse gestures. Your left hand steers. Keep both visible; mirroring on is recommended.

Hold the left palm still for about 0.3 seconds with at least three fingers extended to set a comfortable center. No initial fist is needed. Move that palm as a small joystick:

Left handMovement
Forward / left / back / right from centerW / A / S / D held
DiagonalTwo keys, such as W + A
Return to centerStop movement
Close fistStop immediately; reopen to set a new center

Joystick travel × palm: lower needs less movement; higher gives more neutral room. Hand loss releases the corresponding input; missing right hand or window changes release all input. Hold a steady palm to recenter after interruption. The optional status display shows keys and centering/tracking messages over other apps without taking focus. Ctrl+Alt+M pauses gaming; the two-palm pause gesture is inactive here. The complete joystick path passed actual Blender 5.2.1 Walk Navigation with synthetic hand observations; physical recognition still needs your trial.

Drawing speed ×: a separate constant scale for drawing. Equal camera-pixel travel gives equal screen-pixel travel in every direction, after smoothing. No drawing acceleration, scroll, right pinch click or Wispr gesture. After lost tracking or a window change, show index + middle to re-arm.

Try a cross, circle, square and disconnected short strokes. Comfort and physical precision need your trial. Webcam drawing has no pressure or tilt sensor.

Left hand clicks / right hand draws is optional and off by default. When enabled before activation, right index + middle hovers and right index alone draws. Left thumb/index pinch clicks tools; keep another left finger extended and open the pinch briefly to arm. Clicks lift ink and freeze the cursor until release. Only the right fingertip supplies drawing coordinates, smoothing and scale.

Pause, background and stop

Two open palms held about one second: pause/resume Desktop and Drawing. Ctrl+Alt+M: pause/resume every touchpad sub-mode, including Gaming. Lower the palms before repeating. Escape or Stop everything: stop the camera and release app-owned keys/buttons.

Keep running when minimized works for enabled hand touchpad with its background option and a visible tray or floating preview. Other modes can keep an explicitly enabled camera preview while their input stops. Use the tray menu to show SlateInk, pause, stop or quit. Disable controls before changing settings; preferences save locally. Focus changes lift drawing ink and release gaming input.

Use front lighting and keep control fingertips and knuckles in view. Tracking and frames stay local. Native input cannot bypass Windows privilege protection.
