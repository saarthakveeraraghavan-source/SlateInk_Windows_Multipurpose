Swipe to change windows

In Touchpad: desktop, use the relaxed sideways air strokes in your latest recordings. Next: sweep toward the center as in Next_Swipe-Gesture.mp4. Previous: sweep back the other way as in Previous_Swipe-Gesture.mp4. You do not need an open palm, straight fingers or a deliberate wrist turn. Mirrored and unmirrored previews preserve the demonstrated actions.

Let the hand settle momentarily between strokes; recognition arms automatically in about 0.1 seconds. Folded hands can also swipe. Each stroke fires once, followed by the adjustable 0.8-second cooldown. A stationary hand or vertical arm movement does not switch. There is no required finish-pose hold. Pointer actions pause during a recognized swipe; return to upright joined index + middle to point again. Two open palms still pause.

Windows cycle in a stable order, including minimized windows. New windows join the end; closed windows leave. SlateInk, desktop/taskbar, tool windows, disabled windows and windows hidden on other virtual desktops are excluded. Swipes do not launch apps or change workspaces.

Uncheck Enable window swipes to disable next/previous gestures. This works while mouse controls are active, cancels a pending swipe and keeps the mouse enabled. Your choice is saved for later launches; recheck to enable swipes. Disable controls before adjusting Swipe sensitivity × (higher needs less sideways travel) or Swipe cooldown. Swipes are inactive in Gaming and Drawing and cannot interrupt clicks, drags, physical mouse buttons, keyboard modifiers or active dictation. Brief tracking gaps up to 160 ms can be bridged during a reserved stroke, with input frozen; switching requires real hand observations. Longer loss, stale frames, confident ambiguity and large wrist jumps cancel. Low-confidence duplicate detections are ignored.

Tracking safety, two-palm pause, active dictation and helper V gestures retain priority. A transient V-like estimate during a natural control-hand stroke cannot start dictation. Windows activation uses a brief Alt press with immediate release and the standard foreground API. If Windows does not confirm the switch, hand control stops without automatic retry.

Gaming

Choose Touchpad: gaming. Your physical right hand uses the existing mouse gestures. Your left hand steers. Keep both visible; mirroring on is recommended.

Hold the left palm still for about 0.3 seconds with at least three fingers extended to set a comfortable center. No initial fist is needed. Move that palm as a small joystick:

Left handMovement
Forward / left / back / right from centerW / A / S / D held
DiagonalTwo keys, such as W + A
Return to centerStop movement
Close fistStop immediately; reopen to set a new center

Joystick travel × palm: lower needs less movement; higher gives more neutral room. Hand loss releases the corresponding input; missing right hand or window changes release all input. Hold a steady palm to recenter after interruption. The optional status display shows keys and centering/tracking messages over other apps without taking focus. Ctrl+Alt+M pauses gaming; the two-palm pause gesture is inactive here. The complete joystick path passed actual Blender 5.2.1 Walk Navigation with synthetic hand observations; physical recognition still needs your trial.

Drawing speed ×: a separate constant scale for drawing. Equal camera-pixel travel gives equal screen-pixel travel in every direction, after smoothing. No drawing acceleration, scroll, right pinch click or Wispr gesture. After lost tracking or a window change, show index + middle to re-arm.

Try a cross, circle, square and disconnected short strokes. Comfort and physical precision need your trial. Webcam drawing has no pressure or tilt sensor.

Left hand clicks / right hand draws is optional and off by default. When enabled before activation, right index + middle hovers and right index alone draws. Left thumb/index pinch clicks tools; keep another left finger extended and open the pinch briefly to arm. Clicks lift ink and freeze the cursor until release. Only the right fingertip supplies drawing coordinates, smoothing and scale.

Pause, background and stop

Two open palms held about one second: pause/resume Desktop and Drawing. Ctrl+Alt+M: pause/resume every touchpad sub-mode, including Gaming. Lower the palms before repeating. Escape or Stop everything: stop the camera and release app-owned keys/buttons.

Keep running when minimized works only for an enabled hand touchpad with its tray indicator. Use the tray menu to show SlateInk, pause, stop or quit. Disable controls before changing settings; preferences save locally. Focus changes lift drawing ink and release gaming input.

Use front lighting and keep control fingertips and knuckles in view. Tracking and frames stay local. Native input cannot bypass Windows privilege protection.
